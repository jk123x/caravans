# Caravan/RV Market Research - February 2026

Deep competitive and market research for the caravan/RV digital products opportunity.

## Key Findings

### The Gap
The $15-49 digital product range is basically empty. Below: cheap Etsy junk ($3-7). Above: physical books ($65-115). Nobody selling well-designed, Australia-specific digital guides in between.

### Market Size
- 901,000+ registered RVs in Australia
- 15.2 million caravan/camping trips per year
- $14 billion annual spend
- 400,000+ Facebook group members across major groups
- US market is 8-10x larger ($35.66B USD)

## Etsy Landscape

- Caravan printables priced $3-$12 AUD
- Low sales volumes across the board. No runaway bestsellers
- Products are generic templates, not Australia-specific
- No regional guides, no technical guides
- Quality is inconsistent
- **The category is underpopulated, not oversaturated**

## Amazon Kindle

- Multiple caravan travel books ($5-$60)
- Common review complaints: don't cover enough of Australia, outdated info, not enough practical detail, too expensive for what you get
- Collyn Rivers' electrical books are the technical authority ($30-$60)

## Product Opportunity Analysis

### Regional Trip Guides
- **Demand:** HIGH
- **Gap:** MODERATE-HIGH (free blog content exists, but no purchasable comprehensive regional guides at $15-25)
- **Best regions for first products:** Far North QLD, Western Australia, The Kimberley, Tasmania
- Positioned as curation + planning, not competing with WikiCamps' location database

### Checklists and Planners
- **Demand:** MODERATE-HIGH
- **Gap:** LOW standalone (generic ones exist on Etsy)
- **Best as:** Lead magnet, upsell, or bundle component. Not standalone products

### Technical Guides (Solar, Electrical, Water)
- **Demand:** VERY HIGH. Solar questions dominate every group
- **Gap:** HIGH. Collyn Rivers exists but is dense and technical. No beginner-friendly digital guide
- **Potential strongest single product opportunity**

### Free Camping Guides (by region)
- **Demand:** VERY HIGH
- **Gap:** LOW for static location lists (WikiCamps owns this). HIGH for curated "best of" with context and itineraries
- **Risk:** Information goes stale quickly

### Budget Planners
- **Demand:** MODERATE-HIGH
- **Gap:** MODERATE (Margie and Tim sell one for $10, few free templates exist)
- **Best format:** Interactive spreadsheet + companion PDF. $15-25

### First-Time Buyer Guide
- **Demand:** MODERATE-HIGH
- **Gap:** MODERATE (dealer guides exist but biased)
- **Risk:** Potentially controversial (naming brands with issues). One-time purchase

## US Market

- 8.1M RV-owning households, 16.9M planning to buy
- $35.66B market
- More developed digital product space (RV LIFE Pro $50-65/yr, Campendium, The Dyrt)
- Etsy much more saturated (5,000+ listings)
- **Import opportunity:** Cell coverage + Starlink viability data overlaid on free camping locations. Nobody does this in AU

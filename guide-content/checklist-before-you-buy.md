## Your Gear

☐ Filled in the Shopping List worksheet with specific products and prices

☐ Battery is **lithium** with a **built-in BMS**

☐ Inverter is **pure sine wave**

☐ Included a **battery monitor** (e.g. Victron SmartShunt)

☐ Included an **external Anderson plug socket** for your portable panel

☐ Included **cables, fuses, and connectors** in your budget

## Will It Fit?

☐ Measured your **roof space** — panels fit with room around A/C unit, antenna, and hatches

☐ Checked your **battery box** — the new battery physically fits inside it

☐ Checked the **weight** — lithium is usually lighter than AGM, but worth confirming

## Quick Sizing Check

☐ Solar watts ≈ 1.5-2× your battery Ah? (e.g. 200Ah battery → 300-400W solar)

☐ Controller amps ≈ 1/3 of your solar watts? (e.g. 400W solar → 30-40A controller)

☐ Inverter handles your biggest appliance with room to spare?

If you bought from the shopping lists in Section 4, these will all check out — they're pre-balanced.

## Installation

☐ Got an installer lined up? A qualified auto electrician with caravan experience will have this done in a day. Hand them the **Installer Brief** from this guide — it has everything they need.

☐ Going DIY? Grab the manufacturer's installation guides for each component. For anything beyond a straightforward like-for-like swap, an auto electrician is the way to go — it's a day's work and you'll know it's done right.

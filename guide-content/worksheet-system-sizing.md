## Quick Cross-Check

Does your system pass these sanity checks?

☐ Solar watts ≈ 1.5-2× battery Ah? (e.g., 200Ah → 300-400W)

☐ Controller amps ≈ 1/3 of solar watts? (e.g., 400W → 30-40A)

☐ Inverter watts ≈ 2-3× your biggest 240V appliance?

☐ All three checks pass? Your system is balanced. Go buy it.

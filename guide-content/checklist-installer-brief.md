## Why This Page Exists

Installers are busy. If you walk in and say "I want solar on my caravan," they'll give you whatever system they normally install or whatever brand they're partnered with. That might be perfect for you, or it might be more expensive than you need, or it might not be enough.

You've done the homework. You know what you need. This page makes sure that's what you get.

## Questions to Ask Your Installer

These help you make sure you're getting a quality job.

1. **"Have you installed this brand/setup before?"** — Experience with your specific gear matters. If they haven't, that's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

2. **"Is there anything in my spec list you'd change, and why?"** — A good installer might have a better suggestion. Listen to their reasoning. But if they just want to swap everything for the brand they stock, push back.

3. **"What's included in the quote and what's extra?"** — Wiring, mounting brackets, fuses, and labour should all be itemised. Watch for vague quotes that don't break down what you're paying for.

4. **"How long will it take?"** — A standard tourer system is typically a day's work. If someone quotes you three days, ask why.

5. **"What warranty do I get on the installation work?"** — The components have their own manufacturer warranties. The installation labour should have a separate warranty from the installer.

## After Installation: Walk-Through Checklist

Before the installer leaves, get them to show you:

☐ How to read the battery monitor: what the percentage means, what charging looks like

☐ Where the main fuse or isolator is, so you can switch the system off if you ever need to

☐ How to connect the portable panel via the Anderson plug

☐ That the system is currently working: solar is charging, battery level is going up

☐ Test one of your 240V appliances through the inverter

☐ Their contact number in case anything seems off while you're on the road
